# Hasard (Chance)

Four ways to let chance decide: a wheel with your own choices, dice, heads or tails, and a random number between two limits.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## The wheel

- Write your choices in **Wheel choices**, one per line (to start: pizza, sushi, burger, pasta, tacos, salad). The wheel redraws at once, one slice per choice.
- **Click the wheel** or **Spin**: it turns several times while slowing down, then stops on a choice, shown below.

## The dice

Choose **1 to 6 dice** and their number of **sides**: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20 or 100. **Roll** makes them tumble for a moment, then shows each die and the **total**. Six-sided dice show their pips, the others their number.

## Heads or tails

**Toss the coin** spins it, then shows **Heads** or **Tails**, with a one-in-two chance.

## Random number

Enter a minimum and a maximum (**From** … to …), then **Draw**: a whole number between the two, limits included.

## Data and sync

Your wheel choices and settings are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Hasard → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
